Backdated Sale Conflicts

Backdated sales that included an item already physically counted in a Stock Count covering that date. By default the stock effect for those item(s) is skipped automatically — that count's number already reflects them being gone, so deducting again would double it. The sale itself is always recorded normally either way. Rows marked force-deducted are the exception — an admin/manager deliberately overrode the skip — those are the ones worth a second look.
